# Dolci Vita website completion plan

**Review date:** 24 September 2026
**Reviewed against:** the supplied “Dolci Vita — Website Refresh Project Scope” and the current Astro codebase.

## Review note

The preview URL could not be fetched from the review environment: both the browser service and direct HTTP requests were rejected upstream (`401` and `403` respectively). The codebase does, however, identify that URL as its production site and contains only the two routes generated by the current build. This plan therefore treats the checked-in implementation as the authoritative preview implementation. A final browser, device and performance pass against the deployed URL remains a required release step.

## Executive assessment

The visual foundation is substantially complete: there is a responsive luxury-oriented design system, a media-led hero, menu discovery, best-seller cards, story, gallery, location/map, a filterable menu, descriptive image alternatives, and visible focus/reduced-motion support.

The project is **not content- or feature-complete against the brief**. The largest gap is the core conversion journey: the homepage currently offers telephone ordering and a menu link, but no embedded ordering experience visible on initial load. Only Home and Menu exist; app, recruitment, contact, FAQ, policy, accessibility and individual product pages are absent. Reviews, hygiene rating, app promotion, complete product assets/data, legal navigation, and most technical SEO are also outstanding.

### Minimum required fields

- **`business-details.md`:** official trading/business name, public address exactly as it should appear, phone, public email, collection hours, delivery hours, exceptional-hours process, all social URLs, final production domain, Google Business Profile URL, hygiene-rating value/source URL, and who approves content.
- **`menu.csv`:** one product per row with `slug`, `name`, `category`, `short_description`, `long_description`, `price`, `currency`, `dietary_labels`, `allergen_statement`, `image_filename`, `image_alt`, `badge`, `featured`, `available`, and `order_url_or_provider_id`. Please define whether the displayed price includes all required taxes/fees and how often it changes.
- **`ordering/integration-notes.md`:** the exact Flipdish embed snippet currently used by the existing website, public order URL, widget/store identifier, collection/delivery behaviour, desired CTA wording, allowed-domain configuration and any known consent requirements. Put secret keys in deployment secrets, not this file. If the snippet contains anything that appears to be a secret rather than a public widget ID, redact it and identify the deployment secret name instead.
- **`app-links.md`:** exact Apple App Store and Google Play URLs, supported devices/regions and preferred app-banner wording; accompany these with approved screenshots for both platforms.
- **Photos/media:** original highest-quality files, a short description, intended placement, preferred crop/focal point, photographer/rights confirmation and any credit requirement. Product filenames should match `menu.csv` slugs.
- **`reviews.md`:** approved review excerpts, reviewer display name, rating, date, direct source URL and permission/usage basis, plus Google reviews integration details if a plugin/API is required.
- **Policy files:** final client/legal-adviser-approved text, effective date, business/contact details and the actual analytics, map, review, form, ordering and marketing services that must be disclosed.
- **Page copy:** approved headings/body/CTA destination for FAQ, Work With Us and Contact, including the job application method and contact-form recipient/retention expectations.

8. Are dietary labels informational only, and what exact allergen disclaimer/process has the business approved?

## Recommended plan of action

### Phase 0 — unblock decisions and collect final content

Before building further, obtain and sign off:

1. **Flipdish embed details:** exact widget snippet from the existing site, canonical order URL, public store/widget identifiers, collection/delivery behaviour, domain restrictions, consent requirements and supported tracking events.
2. **Final catalogue:** product names, categories, descriptions, prices, dietary/allergen wording, availability, best-seller flags and a stable image per item.
3. **App assets:** Apple App Store and Google Play URLs, approved badges, device screenshots and platform availability.
4. **Brand/location assets:** team photo, shop-front photo, gallery/slideshow media and permission to use them.
5. **Trust data:** Google Business Profile/reviews integration access and the current hygiene-rating source/URL.
6. **Business copy:** verified story/quality claims, contact destination, recruitment process, FAQs, address, phone, opening/delivery hours and social URLs.
7. **Legal copy and consent requirements:** final Terms, Privacy, Cookie and Accessibility text, plus which analytics/marketing services will be enabled.

Record these in a content sheet and name an owner for updates. Do not launch placeholder prices, draft copy or unverifiable claims.

### Phase 1 — fix the conversion path (highest priority)

1. Put the actual ordering experience in the first viewport of the homepage, with a resilient direct-order fallback if the third-party embed fails.
2. Replace every dead `#` ordering/gallery link and make “Order now” consistently open the agreed ordering flow rather than defaulting to a phone call.
3. Add a clear order CTA to featured cards, full-menu cards and later product pages.
4. Keep the current hero for discovery but size the hero/order area so ordering is genuinely visible on load on common laptop and mobile viewports.
5. Add conversion analytics only after consent, covering order CTA clicks, embed starts, completed hand-off, app-store clicks, calls and form submissions.

**Acceptance criteria:** a first-time mobile or desktop visitor can begin a real collection/delivery order without opening site navigation; all order CTAs resolve to the same maintained journey; embed failure never produces a dead end.

### Phase 2 — complete the homepage

1. Remove all editorial placeholders and replace them with approved content.
2. Add Google reviews/social proof and the hygiene rating, with source links and a no-JavaScript/static fallback.
3. Add the non-intrusive app banner, initially rather than a modal.
4. Finish gallery destinations and final imagery.
5. Finish the contact/location section, including a titled map, genuine delivery link and shop-front image where it best supports wayfinding.
6. Expand the footer into the complete site map and correct copy/link defects.

**Acceptance criteria:** every homepage CTA works, every claim is approved, every brief section is present, and no placeholder/draft string is visible.

### Phase 3 — make Menu a complete, crawlable catalogue

1. Replace placeholder prices and missing photography; validate all data against the approved catalogue.
2. Add an explicit legend/disclaimer for dietary and allergen data, and a route for customers to ask before ordering.
3. Create product slugs and dedicated static product routes with unique title, meta description, H1, product details, imagery and order CTA.
4. Turn featured/menu cards into meaningful links while keeping category filtering progressively usable.
5. Fix the invalid nested main landmark and verify focus behaviour after filtering.

**Acceptance criteria:** every listed product has complete approved data, its own indexable URL and a working order path; category filters work with keyboard and pointer input; rendered HTML has one main landmark and one H1.

### Phase 4 — build the remaining scoped pages

Implement in this order so the highest customer value ships first:

1. **Contact** — validated, accessible form; contact details; map; opening hours; shop-front image; useful success/error states.
2. **Download App** — Android/iOS store links, platform sections and high-quality screenshots; connect the homepage banner.
3. **FAQs** — approved answers, accessible disclosure pattern if used, and eligible schema.
4. **Work With Us** — existing/adapted content, team imagery and a clear application CTA.
5. **Supporting pages** — Terms, Privacy, Cookie Policy, Accessibility and persistent Cookie Settings.

Use the existing design tokens/components so these read as one system rather than bespoke microsites.

### Phase 5 — SEO, local discovery and sharing

1. Extend the shared layout to accept canonical URL, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, social image and indexing controls.
2. Add validated local-business structured data and only use product/offer or FAQ schema where its requirements are fully met.
3. Generate sitemap XML and `robots.txt`; include every public page/product and exclude non-production/utility URLs.
4. Strengthen internal links among Home, Menu, products, FAQ, Contact and app download pages.
5. Confirm one descriptive H1 per page, logical heading order, descriptive links and concise, unique metadata.
6. Configure redirects/canonicals when moving from the preview domain to `dolcivita.co.uk`, then register the production sitemap with search tooling.

### Phase 6 — accessibility, privacy, quality and launch assurance

1. Perform keyboard-only and screen-reader journeys for navigation, carousel, menu filters, order embed, app banner, cookie controls and contact form.
2. Support 200%/400% zoom and reflow; test colour contrast, target size, focus visibility, error identification and reduced motion.
3. Provide a pause mechanism for autoplaying motion, prevent background/decorative media from creating noise, and provide a useful fallback when video cannot play.
4. Gate non-essential review/map/analytics/marketing services as required by the approved cookie policy; ensure Cookie Settings can withdraw consent.
5. Optimise media: responsive image dimensions/formats, explicit width/height, sensible lazy loading, poster/fallback images and less aggressive video preload.
6. Test the deployed production build across current Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge on representative iOS/Android/desktop sizes.
7. Run automated HTML/accessibility/link checks plus Lighthouse on Home, Menu, one product and Contact, then manually verify all forms and external hand-offs.

**Launch gate:** no dead links, placeholders, console errors or serious accessibility findings; all forms, order and app links are verified; metadata/schema/sitemap validate; performance targets are agreed and met on representative mobile connectivity.

## Definition of done

The refresh is ready for approval when:

- the full scoped route set exists and is reachable through navigation/footer links;
- ordering is immediately visible and functional on the homepage;
- all products have approved content, imagery, pricing, individual pages and ordering CTAs;
- reviews, hygiene rating, app promotion, contact/map, gallery and footer are complete;
- client-supplied legal pages and functional cookie settings are present;
- titles, descriptions, canonicals, social metadata, appropriate schema, sitemap and robots rules are validated;
- accessibility and responsive QA has no unresolved critical/serious issue;
- all client-owned claims, prices, hours, links and assets have written approval; and
- a production-domain smoke test confirms order, form, app, map, social and cookie flows work end to end.
